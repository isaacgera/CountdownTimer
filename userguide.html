Countdown Timer

User Guide — v1.2.0

What it does

Countdown Timer keeps a tidy list of the dates you're waiting for — birthdays, deadlines, trips, anniversaries — and shows how many days are left for each, updating as the days tick by. Everything stays on your device; there are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere.

Getting around

There are two lists and an add screen:

  • Upcoming — events still ahead, nearest first (the default view).
  • Past — events that have gone by.
  • Add — the form for creating a new countdown.

On a phone these sit in the bar along the bottom of the screen; on a larger screen they're buttons at the top right. Each list shows a small count badge.

Adding a countdown

  1. Open Add.
  2. Type the event name (e.g. "Mum's birthday").
  3. Pick a date. It defaults to today, so just change it.
  4. Optionally add a time — leave it blank for an all-day event.
  5. Choose a category, set Repeat if it recurs, and add a short note if you like.
  6. Press Save.
If you add a time, the card also shows the hours and minutes left, not just days.

Repeating events

Set Repeat to Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or Custom (every N days/weeks/months/years). A repeating event always counts down to its next occurrence — so a yearly birthday rolls forward instead of dropping into Past — and is marked with a ↻ badge. Editing one changes its original date; the countdown recalculates.

Reading the cards

  • Days to go — the big number is whole days remaining.
  • Today — the event is today.
  • Soon — within the next 7 days.
  • Passed — the date is in the past; shown as "N days ago" and dimmed.

Colours are always backed up by words, so the meaning is clear without relying on colour.

Editing & deleting

  • Edit — tap the pencil ✎ on a card. It opens in the Add screen; press Save (or Cancel).
  • Delete — tap the bin 🗑. You'll be asked to confirm first. Deleting can't be undone (unless you restore from a backup).

Sorting & paging

  • Sort (top-right of a list): Nearest first (default), Furthest first, by name, or by category. "Nearest" always means closest to today — so in Past it's the most recent event.
  • Paging: lists show 10 at a time. If there are more, arrows and a "page X of Y" appear next to the sort control. Fewer than that and there's no pager.

Backup, restore & moving your data

  • Export (⭳ in the header) saves all your events to a JSON file.
  • Import (⭱) reads a backup back in. It merges — matching events update, new ones are added, nothing is wiped.
The backup format is shared with the upcoming Event Planner app, so events you start here will be able to carry over into a fuller plan later — same data, no retyping.

Personalise (colour & theme)

Tap the palette button (🎨) in the header to open Personalise. Pick a light or dark mode and one of eight colour themes (Blue, Teal, Green, Amber, Orange, Crimson, Purple, Magenta). Colour and mode are independent, and both are remembered on this device. It follows your device's light/dark setting the first time.

Installing & offline

Open the app in your browser and choose Install (or "Add to Home Screen" on mobile) to run it like a normal app. Once loaded it works offline — your events are stored on the device.

Tip for testing: open it through a local server or its hosted URL rather than a file:// path, so install and offline behave correctly.

Your data & privacy

Everything lives in your browser's local storage on this device only. No sign-in, no server, no tracking. Clearing your browser data for the site will remove your events, so keep an export if they matter.
